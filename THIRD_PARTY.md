## Linked content and map services

YouTube videos and thumbnails are linked or loaded from YouTube; no video files are redistributed. Original titles/channels and provider/organization sources remain in `site/videos.js`.

Short operator-policy quotations are labeled as quotations with adjacent source links. Project summaries are distinct from the original wording.

Map imagery is requested on demand from third-party services; no tile datasets are redistributed. Preserve all displayed attribution and applicable provider terms:

- [OpenStreetMap tiles](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/), ©OpenStreetMap contributors.
- [Esri World Imagery](https://services.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer), Source: Esri, Vantor, Earthstar Geographics and the GIS User Community.
- [OSRM public routing service](https://github.com/Project-OSRM/osrm-backend/wiki/Demo-server), OpenStreetMap data, service sponsored by FOSSGIS. Best effort, modest non-commercial use, maximum one request/second.
- [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/), weather model API with attribution and original-query links.
